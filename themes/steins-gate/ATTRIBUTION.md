# Steins;Gate

Steins;Gate shares Bee's Theme's direct renderer, `createView(options, true)`
from `../jl/theme.js`, so it has the same group tabs, formatted definitions,
image previews, personal dictionary editor, custom actions and Design settings.
Only the stylesheet differs. It starts from Bee's stylesheet and recolours it:
nixie-tube amber headwords with a soft glow, lab-coat cream text, pale gold
readings, teal dictionary names and brass borders on a warm black background
with faint CRT scanlines. A decorative divergence meter reading `1.048596`
sits above the tabs; its alternative text is empty, its brief warm-up flicker
is skipped under reduced motion, and forced colours turn off the glow.

The layout follows JL; see [JL's attribution](../jl/ATTRIBUTION.md) and
[Apache-2.0 licence](../jl/LICENSE.Apache-2.0). Hachidori integration is
GPL-3.0-or-later.

This is an unofficial fan theme inspired by the visual novel Steins;Gate. It is
not affiliated with or endorsed by MAGES. or its licensors, and it contains no
artwork, logos, fonts or other assets from the work: only CSS colours and a
number.
